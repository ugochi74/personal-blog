# Start Before You Feel Completely Ready

Waiting until you feel completely confident can keep a good idea permanently in the planning stage. Confidence often grows after you begin, practise, and learn from the result.

Starting does not mean acting carelessly. It means taking a small, sensible first step while accepting that you will still have things to learn.

## Turn a large goal into a first action

If you want to become a software engineer, do not focus only on the final job title. Your first step might be writing a simple program.

If you want to write a book, begin with one page or a short outline.

If you want to improve your health, choose a safe activity that fits your current ability and schedule.

## A simple action plan

1. Write down the goal.
2. Identify the smallest useful next action.
3. Decide when you will do it.
4. Complete the action before adding unnecessary complexity.
5. Review what happened and choose the next step.

You may make mistakes. You may need to change your plan. That is part of doing meaningful work.

Motivation can help you begin, but routines, support, and realistic planning help you continue.

**Remember:** You do not have to know every step before taking the first one. You do need to take the next step thoughtfully.
